🎯 Evaluation Criteria Breakdown & Scoring Alignment
In the HackerRank Orchestrate challenge, your submission (output.csv) is evaluated on 6 key scoring dimensions. Here is how our system computes, validates, and optimizes each dimension:

1. Accuracy of amount_safe_to_pay
Definition: The maximum cash the user can pay on request_date without breaching their minimum_balance_to_keep at any point over the 90-day simulation.
Mathematical Formula: $$\text{amount_safe_to_pay} = \min\left(\text{requested_amount}, \max\left(0, \min_{t \in [0, 90]} (\text{balance}_t - \text{minimum_balance})\right)\right)$$
How Our Engine Guarantees Accuracy:
Simulates the exact day-by-day cash balance across all 91 days ($t = 0 \dots 90$).
Detects recurring income (salary on the 15th) and debits (rent on the 1st, utilities on the 8th, weekly groceries, subscriptions).
Normalizes foreign currency cash flows to the user's home_currency using dated exchange rates from exchange_rates.csv.
Ensures the invariant $0 \le \text{amount_safe_to_pay} \le \text{requested_amount}$ is strictly satisfied.
2. Correctness of affordability_status
Allowed Values: affordable_now, affordable_with_plan, affordable_later, not_affordable.
Decision Logic:
affordable_now: Full amount is safe today ($\text{safe} \ge \text{requested}$) AND user accepts full_payment.
affordable_with_plan: Full amount is completed before desired_completion_date using:
An eligible installment option from request_payment_options.csv, OR
A 2-part partial payment schedule, OR
Permitted flexible spending cuts (stopping/reducing subscriptions).
affordable_later: Full payment is not safe today, but becomes safe on or before desired_completion_date (e.g. once confirmed salary arrives).
not_affordable: Full amount cannot be completed safely within the 90-day window without violating the safety floor.
3. Correctness of recommended_payment_method and payment_plan
Allowed Methods: full_payment, partial_payment, installments, wait, not_recommended.
Required Ranking Order (Tie-breaker hierarchy):
Complete the full request by desired_completion_date.
Require no spending changes.
Minimize the total amount paid (including financing fees).
Start payment earlier.
Use fewer payments.
Lowest payment_option_id as the final tie-breaker.
Payment Plan Formatting:
Valid: YYYY-MM-DD:amount|YYYY-MM-DD:amount in strict chronological order.
If not affordable: none.
For partial_payment: Exactly 2 payments (pay amount_safe_to_pay on request_date, remainder on earliest_date_for_full_payment).
4. Accuracy of earliest_date_for_full_payment
Capacity Measurement: Measures pure financial capacity independently of user method preferences.
Rules:
For affordable_now: Must equal request_date.
For affordable_later: Earliest date $d$ where single full payment on $d$ keeps all 90 days $\ge \text{minimum_balance}$.
If not safe within 90 days: Left blank ("").
5. Validity of spending_changes_needed
Format: stop:<event_id> or reduce_to:<event_id>:<new_amount> (up to 3 changes separated by |) or none.
Rules:
Only flexible recurring expenses (stoppable, reducible, reducible_or_stoppable) are eligible.
Categories must match user's expense_categories_user_is_willing_to_stop or expense_categories_user_is_willing_to_reduce.
Stopping and reducing the same event are mutually exclusive.
6. Usefulness and Consistency of decision_explanation
Standardized Style:
State the recommended action and currency amount clearly.
State the supporting reason: margin remaining, arrival of next salary, or breach of buffer.
Free of contradictions with affordability_status and payment_plan.
